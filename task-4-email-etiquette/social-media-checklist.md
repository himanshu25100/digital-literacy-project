## Do's for responsible social media use
Regularly review who can see your content. Maintain a clear distinction between private personal accounts and public professional profiles.

Share your academic projects, volunteer work, and professional achievements to show future employers your value.

Verify Before Sharing: Fact-check news or viral stories before reposting. This builds your reputation as a thoughtful and credible digital citizen.

Use platforms like LinkedIn to be informed about the current career trends and stay on top of all the informations with the help of industry experts.

 Ensure your profile pictures—especially on professional-leaning sites—are clear, appropriate, and represent you well.

## Dont's for responsible social media use
Avoid posting during lectures or internships, as it can signal a lack of focus and poor time management to observers.

Never post negative comments about professors, classmates, or employers. Digital complaints can be easily screenshotted and shared.

Avoid sharing any information that can be classified as "illegal" as these can lead to disciplinary action.

Don’t assume "deleted" content is truly gone. Always be careful of what you share or post on the internet.

Avoid posting your specific real-time location, home address, or daily schedule to protect your physical safety.
